Nairobi Grace Chapel

Mobile Application Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

Nairobi Grace Chapel ("the Church", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, shared and protected when you use the Nairobi Grace Chapel mobile application ("the App").

The App provides access to church services, Christian teachings, live broadcasts, Bible Study Groups, attendance, pastoral care, giving information and church announcements.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address, where requested.
  • Church attendance and registration information.
  • Bible Study Group registration details.
  • Prayer requests and pastoral care information you submit.
  • Voluntarily provided giving details, including transaction references and giving categories.
  • Push notification tokens, device information and technical information necessary for app functionality.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction information that may be processed by advertising providers.

Pastoral care requests may contain sensitive information that you voluntarily disclose. Please share only information necessary for your request.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide church registration and membership services.
  • Manage Bible Study Groups and attendance records.
  • Respond to prayer requests and pastoral care inquiries.
  • Communicate church announcements and service updates.
  • Send notifications when permission is granted.
  • Support voluntary giving and related administration.
  • Maintain the App's security, functionality and reliability.
  • Display church announcements, sponsored content and ads.

We do not sell church membership or pastoral care information to advertisers.

4. Live Streaming and Video Content

The App provides live and recorded Christian content. Videos may be delivered using YouTube or other streaming providers. These services may process technical and usage information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

The App may display church announcements, sponsored messages and advertising through Google AdMob. Advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers and information about ad interactions, subject to applicable permissions and privacy requirements.

For further information, see Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Payments and Donations

Donations and offerings are voluntary. Payments may be handled by external payment providers, including mobile money and payment-processing services where enabled.

The Church does not request your M-Pesa PIN. Payment providers may process transaction information under their own privacy policies. Voluntarily submitted giving records may be retained for financial administration and applicable legal obligations.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • Authorized church staff responsible for membership, pastoral care, administration, broadcasting or finance.
  • Service providers supporting databases, notifications, video delivery, payments or advertising.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Access to church records is intended to be limited to authorized responsibilities.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration and disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for church administration, operational purposes and applicable legal obligations.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting the Church using the details below. Verified requests will be reviewed and processed subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

The App includes Christian educational content for Sunday School and teenagers. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of the App.

Children should not independently submit personal details or pastoral care requests without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. Where required by applicable law, parental or guardian consent will be sought before knowingly processing children's personal information.

11. Push Notifications

Users may choose whether to allow push notifications about church services, live broadcasts and announcements. Notification permissions can be changed in device settings.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where appropriate, or information about how your data is used. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

13. Third-Party Services

The App may use Supabase for backend services, Expo for app infrastructure, Google Firebase for notification delivery, Google AdMob for advertising, YouTube and other video providers, and payment providers including IntaSend where enabled.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, legal requirements or data-processing practices change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage with a revised effective date.
